    15→//          quick-action status buttons (Confirm / Pack / Ship / Deliver),
    16→//          invoice + shipping-label downloads.

    18→//          Mobile: card sections stack vertically; quick actions collapse

   432→        trackingNumber: parsed.trackingNumber || "",
   433→        carrier: parsed.carrier || "",
